# Risks

Draft for Gate 0, 15 November 2026. Ordered by how likely each is to end or badly
damage the project. Reviewed at every gate.

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Early sign | What we do about it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The project stalls from lack of time.** Six hours a week, no game-development experience, a long road to anything playable. | High | Fatal | Two weeks with no commit. A phase running at double its estimate. | Gate 1 is time-boxed to twelve weeks with a forced review. Tasks are small enough to finish in one sitting. The backlog is always ordered. |
| 2 | **The core loop is not fun.** Pinning and flanking with circles may feel flat. | Medium | Fatal | Gate 1 criteria 2 or 5 fail. | Five written pass criteria agreed in advance. A thin playable loop comes first so feel is tested from week two, not week eight. |
| 3 | **Invented history gets in.** Claude fills a gap with plausible detail, or a page number from memory. It already produced two wrong recollections in the first session. | High | High | A name, unit, date or citation appears with no entry in `docs/history/`. | Nothing enters game text unless read in a source that session or checked by Amit. Grey-box content uses obviously fake labels. `TO_VERIFY.md` records every unconfirmed claim. |
| 4 | **Pillar 1 is not historical.** If an INA section had no light machine gun, "one team pins" may be a fantasy. | Medium | High | Sources confirm rifles only. | Section weapons are data. The prototype is played both ways. If rifles-only is the truth, suppression becomes weak and expensive, which strengthens pillar 3. |
| 5 | **The history rests on one article.** S1 leans on British intelligence summaries and argues the INA was a "paper tiger". | High | Medium | A second source contradicts S1. Seven conflicts are already listed. | Get Shah Nawaz Khan's memoir and Fay. With no budget before Gate 1, that means a library or a loan. Everything stays provisional until then. |
| 6 | **The Windows build is never run on Windows.** Development is on an 8 GB Mac with no Windows machine. | High | High | Gate 3 approaches with no real Windows test. | CI builds the Windows export. The first real run is on a borrowed PC before Gate 3. This is the biggest untested technical assumption. |
| 7 | **Scope creeps in through "data-driven".** General mission and dialogue systems get built before one mission is fun. | Medium | High | A feature that serves a second mission. A file format with no user yet. | Tuning numbers go in data from day one. Mission scripting and dialogue formats wait until a real mission needs them. |
| 8 | **The old vision pulls the project off course.** The earlier notes describe an Unreal 3D shooter with tanks and voice-over, and the concept art sells that game. | Medium | High | Requests for 3D, set pieces, or features from the earlier notes. | Hard constraints stand until changed in writing. `docs/SCOPE.md` lists what was parked and why. |
| 9 | **CI has never run.** The workflow is written and its parts pass locally on macOS, but it has not run on GitHub or on Linux. | High (first push) | Low | A red first run. | Fix on first push. Expect one or two rounds. |
| 10 | **The simulation gives different results on macOS and Linux.** Floating-point differences would break seeded tests in CI. | Low | Medium | A seeded test passes locally and fails in CI. | Keep simulation maths integer or simple. Add a cross-platform golden-value test with the first simulation code. |
| 11 | **The title clashes with a known book.** *Beyond the Chindwin* is Bernard Fergusson's 1945 memoir of the first Chindit expedition, a British account of crossing the river the other way. | Certain | Low to medium | Search results and reviews confuse the two. | Amit's call. No game of that name was found on Steam or itch.io. Trademark and store-name checks happen in Phase 4. |
| 12 | **The subject draws hostile attention.** Some will object to showing desertion and the Japanese alliance; others to anything that reads as praise of an Axis-allied force. Maps of 1944 borders can also be sensitive in India. | Medium | Medium | Angry responses to the first public material. | Source every claim. Tag every scene. Show no side as a cartoon. Get a legal view on maps before the store page. |
| 13 | **The concept art cannot ship.** It is AI-generated, its licence is not established, and its historical details are invented. | Certain | Low | It appears on a public page. | Logged in `ASSETS.md` as placeholder and reference only. Steam's rules on AI-generated content are to be checked in Phase 4. |
| 14 | **No art skill and no budget.** | Certain | Medium | Phase 3 arrives with no affordable style. | A map-and-counters style keeps the gap from grey box to final art small. Decided in Phase 3. |
| 15 | **The repository exists on one laptop.** | Certain until pushed | High | n/a | Amit creates the private GitHub repository and pushes. First item in the backlog. |
| 16 | **Tooling drifts.** Godot 4.8 is in beta. The linter was last released in October 2025 and may not parse newer syntax. | Medium | Low | Lint fails on valid code. | Engine version is pinned with checksums and changes only between phases. If the linter breaks, drop it; the compile check is the real guard. |
| 17 | **Hindi arrives late and breaks the layout.** Devanagari needs a proper font and more room than English. | Medium | Low | Phase 3 text overflows. | All text is behind keys from Phase 1. No tiny pixel fonts. |
